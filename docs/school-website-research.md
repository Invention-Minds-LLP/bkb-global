# School Website Research: Best Designs and Animation for BKB Global

As of 31 October 2026 · Online version: https://claude.ai/artifact/2EK89pRGUxdWxDCwjqjbFw

## Summary

Woodleigh School (Australia) is the strongest model for BKB Global: its animation tells the story of school life, yet daily information stays one tap away. Wellington College (UK) sets the ceiling for ambition, and Korowa (Australia) shows how small motion can present facts.

The best school sites share three habits: motion that explains the school, real photography of real students, and a "book a visit" route that stays in reach on every screen.

## Lessons for the BKB Global homepage

1. **Motion should explain the school.** Woodleigh's illustrated stages and Blundell's numbered journey tell the story as you scroll.
2. **Animate real facts only.** Count-up numbers need real figures from the school.
3. **Real photos beat effects.** BKB Global needs its own photos or video; stock is a stand-in.
4. **Keep "Book a visit" on every screen.**
5. **Protect the current-parent route.** Calendars, notices and portals one tap away.
6. **Respect reduced motion.** Every animation needs a still fallback.

## Source reliability

Findings come from search results, award listings and agency case studies; open the live sites to judge the motion yourself. Awwwards, the Webbys and the Good Design Awards are independent juries. Finalsite, Spicy Web, School Jotter and Orange MonkE promote work they or their clients built.
