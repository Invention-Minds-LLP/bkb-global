# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Angular (user's choice). No project scaffold exists yet; Angular version, rendering mode (SSR/prerender vs. client-only) and hosting are undecided.

## Users

- **Prospective parents (primary):** families comparing schools, usually on a phone, deciding whether this school is worth a visit. Their job is to build enough trust and understanding to book a visit or send an enquiry.
- **Current parents and students:** returning visitors who need day-to-day information (calendars, notices, links to portals) quickly.

## Product Purpose

The public website of BKB Global, a single school. Success means prospective families book a visit or enquire, and current families find what they need without calling the office.

## Positioning

Open decision: what sets BKB Global apart from nearby schools (curriculum, location, age range, values, results) has not been captured yet. Do not invent it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Primary conversion: **book a visit / enquire**. Every prospective-parent journey should lead there.
- Secondary: a fast route for current parents and students to recurring information.
- Undecided: school location and country, curriculum, age range, languages, admissions process, and which portals or systems exist.

## Brand Commitments

Name: **BKB Global**. No logo or voice guidelines have been provided yet.

User-pinned (2026-10-31): school colours are **burgundy and white**; the site should feel **illustrated and lively** (animated illustrations in the spirit of Woodleigh School), warm but never childish, corporate, or stiff. Stock photography stands in until real school photos arrive and must be replaced. A two-ink textbook direction was tried and rejected for its colours and look.

## Evidence on Hand

None in the repository. No photos, results, accreditations, testimonials, staff details or fees have been supplied. Future work must not fabricate any of these; use clearly marked placeholders until real material arrives.

## Product Principles

1. **Every page earns a visit.** Prospective-parent content builds toward booking a visit or enquiring, without pressure tactics.
2. **Real school, real evidence.** Trust comes from genuine photos, people and facts, never stock claims or invented proof.
3. **Current families get there in two taps.** Recurring information is never buried under marketing.
4. **Motion serves understanding.** Animation should guide attention and convey the school's life, never slow parents down or hide information.
