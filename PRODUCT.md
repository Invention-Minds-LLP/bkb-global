# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Angular 22 (user's choice), static build deployed on Netlify (`netlify.toml`). Replaces the school's current WordPress site at https://bkgglobalschool.com.

## Users

- **Prospective parents (primary):** families comparing schools in and around Sandur and Ballari district, often on a phone, deciding whether to visit or apply. Their job is to trust the school enough to book a visit or start an application.
- **Many of them are senior executives** (CEOs, COOs, MDs of the region's companies; client-confirmed 2026-11-04). They judge a school the way they judge an organisation: leadership, decision-making, accountability, quality systems and future readiness. Present BKG's teaching in those terms, using the school's own words, and give **Mental intelligence** (the "M" of BKG-SMILE) particular prominence.
- **Current parents and students:** returning visitors who need timings, fees, happenings, the blog and mandatory disclosures quickly.

## Product Purpose

The public website of **BKG Global School**, Sandur, a BKG Group institution. Success means families book a visit or apply, and current families find what they need without calling the office.

## Positioning

A CBSE day and residential school for boys and girls in a rural setting that offers "truly international standards within the formal CBSE curriculum", rooted in Indian tradition. The BKG-SMILE framework (Spiritual resilience, Mental intelligence, Infrastructure & facilities, Laboratory-based teaching, Emotional intelligence) and practices such as Aksharabhyasa, Agnihotram and Saraswati Vandana set it apart. The chairman describes the group as a pioneer of digital learning in rural areas.

## Operating Context

- CBSE Affiliation No. 830975. Admits Pre-KG to Class IX; teaches to Class X. Separate residential campus for boys and girls from Class V to X.
- Admission: download the form, pay the application fee online (no cash), entrance test (English, Mathematics, Language, Intelligence; Classes I–IX), interview and medical, then fee payment and kit collection.
- Timings: Pre-primary Mon–Fri 8:30–12:30, all Saturdays off. Class 1 onwards Mon–Fri 8:30–3:30, Sat 8:30–12:30, second Saturdays off. Accounts office 9:00–4:00.
- Fees: school fee to the "BKG Global School" HDFC account; hostel fee and caution deposit to the "BKG Foundation" account; RTGS/NEFT/DD/cheque/card. The current fee page still shows 2024–25.
- Contact: Ballari Road, Krishnanagar, Sandur, Ballari District, Karnataka 583119 · 08395-260477 · 094826 84477 · info@bkgglobalschool.com.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Campus: 17 acres, 3 lakh sq ft built, 4.5-acre playing area (basketball, tennis, swimming pool), science labs, English language lab, smart classrooms, computer lab.
- Safety: 24/7 CCTV, RFID student tracking, GPS-fitted buses with female chaperones, 24/7 ambulance, doctor on call from ANANYA Hospital, vegetarian meals planned by dieticians with produce from the group's farms.
- KKare: academic integration (board syllabus with a competitive approach), technology integration, Grand Parenting Programme.
- Online visit booking is not connected to any backend yet; the form says so and points to the phone numbers.
- Most other pages (about, admissions, fees, academics, life, happenings, contact, mandatory disclosure, blog) still live on the old site; the homepage links to them until they are rebuilt.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **BKG Global School** (not "BKB"). Motto: "Sujnana Samriddhi Sukshema". Tagline on the old site: "Where the learning begins".
- Logo: gold scroll crest around a Sri Yantra with "BKG Global School, Sandur" in a royal-blue serif (`public/images/bkg/logo.png`).
- Colours (user-confirmed 2026-11-03): the school's own navy (logo, blazers) and crest gold.
- Style (client-confirmed 2026-11-03): **professional, in the manner of Bishop Cotton School's website**: photo-led, serif headings, quiet motion, **no illustrations**. The earlier illustrated, lively version was rejected as not professional.
- People: founder Sri B. Kumara Gouda (1924–2009); chairman and managing trustee Sri B. Rudra Gouda; founder Smt. B. Kamalamma; trustees Sri B. Naganagouda and Sri B. K. Basavaraj.

## Product Principles

1. **Every page earns a visit or an application**, without pressure tactics.
2. **Real school, real evidence.** Only the school's own photos, people and facts; never stock claims or invented results.
3. **Current families get there in two taps.** Timings, fees and disclosures are never buried.
4. **Motion serves understanding.** Animation explains the school and never slows parents down.
5. **Tradition and modernity together.** Indian rituals and values sit alongside labs and technology, as the school itself describes.
